2026 Regulation Impact

New technical regulations reshape the competitive order for 2026.

🚗

Smaller Cars

2026 cars are narrower and lighter — down to 768 kg from 798 kg. Shorter wheelbase improves tyre management and increases mechanical responsiveness through slow-speed corners.

⚡

Active Aerodynamics

Manual DRS is abolished. Driver-controlled active aero opens automatically on straights at 290 km/h and adjusts drag in real time, placing greater emphasis on aerodynamic efficiency over raw downforce.

🔋

Hybrid Overhaul

A 50/50 power split between the 1.6L V6 ICE and a massively upgraded ERS delivering 350 kW. Total power output rises to ~1,000 bhp, demanding new thermal and energy management strategies.
